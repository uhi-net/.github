<h3 align="center">Realtime voice for AI agents, at a quarter of the price.</h3>

<p align="center"><b>UHI</b> is an inference network on the GPUs and Macs already in people's homes and in idle mining farms.
It runs any model that fits the hardware, launches with streaming text-to-speech and speech-to-text for voice agents,
guarantees latency with two devices in parallel and cloud fallback, verifies the work, and pays the owner for every job in USDC.</p>

<p align="center"><img src="uhi-og.png" alt="Realtime voice for AI agents, at a quarter of the price." width="720"></p>

| At launch | Who | Why they show up |
|---|---|---|
| **Agent builders** | voice-agent platforms, contact-centre agent teams, developers | realtime voice at about a quarter of premium prices, latency covered by cloud fallback |
| **Device owners** | gaming desktops and laptops, idle GPU mining farms, Apple-silicon Macs | paid per execution in USDC, 70% of every job, net pay published per device class |
| **UHI Labs** | the company | routes, verifies, runs the voices, guarantees the latency; keeps 30% from the first paid job |

Phased in by published gates: independent model providers, independent verifiers, and on-chain governance by the people who do the work.
